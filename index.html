IRS Standard Mileage Rates for Business Driving

The IRS standard mileage rate for business use of a vehicle (car, van, pickup, or panel truck) is indexed up to 72.5¢ – 76¢ per mile, reflecting vehicle maintenance and fuel adjustments. Detailed mileage logs are required to sustain deduction compliance.
